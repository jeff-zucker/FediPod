Act as a Fediverse account

Type an address and you are sent to wherever that account signs in, then come back here signed in as it. Any of these works:

  • A FediPod handle — @jeff@fedipod.net — signs in at your Solid pod
  • Other Fediverse handle — @you@mastodon.social — signs in at your server
  • A WebID — https://you.solidcommunity.net/profile/card#me — signs in at your Solid pod

This demo is created with the fediverse-account library. Once signed in, the demo shows you your profile. The library itself returns an account session with methods to post, reply, like, etc.
