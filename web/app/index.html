FediPod - join the Fediverse from a Solid pod.

Welcome to fedipod.net! By creating an account here, you can get a free Solid pod and Fediverse identity or link to your existing pod/identity. Your fedipod.net account provides you a portal to the Fediverse and filters unverified mail.

There is nothing to install, once you've setup an account, just point any browser at https://fedipod.net and sign in.

Fedipod.net is a FediPod Gateway operated by Jeff Zucker. You can detach from the gateway at any time and transfer your account either to your own FediPod Gateway (easy to set up on a free tier Netlify) or to any Fediverse server. All of your data lives on your pod and your Fediverse identity is yours forever independent of which gateway you attach to.

For more information, see github.com/jeff-zucker/FediPod

Unlock your signing key

Your signing key is stored on your pod, locked under your account password, so nobody who can read your pod — your pod's host included — can post as you. This browser has not opened it yet.
