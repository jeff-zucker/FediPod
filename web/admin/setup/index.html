Setting up

    What kind of Actor?
    Actor's Handle

    The name in your fediverse address. This is permanent. Letters, digits, hyphens, and underscores only.

    Actor's Pod

    The Community Solid Server providers from solidproject.org. A provider that makes suffixed pods gives your address at a gateway instead.

    Password is sent once in return for a credential which is stored locally. The password itself is never stored. You may revoke the credential without changing the password.

    The subdomain of your pod address, it will become https://YOUR-POD-NAME.your-identity-provider

    Leave it empty for your pod's root. This is permanent.

    Your pod has no public type index, the list Solid apps use to find your things. FediPod needs one to record where your account lives. It will be readable by anyone, like your profile.

    Where your address lives

    The gateway answers your address and forwards your mail to your pod.
