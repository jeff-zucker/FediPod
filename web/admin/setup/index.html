    What is this?
    Where it lives

    This becomes the domain half of the address.

    Who it is

    The name in the address. Permanent.

    Shown above the address. Changeable later.

    Where its private data lives

    Your timeline, contacts, blocklist and notifications. Your pod always keeps the public half — your address, your actor, your inbox and your published posts.

    Keeping it here means your pod never holds your timeline or your contact list, and stops being asked to store everything you read. The cost is that this data then has one copy: no second machine can share it, and recovery is only as good as your backups.

    Passwords

    Used once, here on your own machine, to create the account and mint a revocable credential. Never stored, never sent anywhere but your identity provider.

    Only needed if you ever expose this agent beyond your own machine. It turns the instant client login into a real login form.
