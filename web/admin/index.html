      Members

      A group cannot force an unfollow. Muting is declining to carry; ejecting also tells their server it is no longer a member.

        Carried

          Inbox

          Your pod accepts deliveries whether or not this agent is running, so a machine that has been off collects them. The agent is working through them oldest first and will get there on its own — this is only here if you would rather not wait.

          Discarding drops posts, not bookkeeping: follows, unfollows, acceptances and deletions in that period are still applied, so your follower list stays right and nothing stays up that its author took down. What goes is the timeline you would have read that week.

          Post moderation 

          


          
  
          
    
          Everyone you follow gets unfollowed — that is what stops posts arriving — and the
      inbox closes, so deliveries are refused outright rather than stored. Your followers keep
      following you and are told nothing. The handle keeps resolving; posts and RDF stay where
      they are. The follow graph is saved first, so Revive can undo this.
          
  
          
  
          
    
          The inbox opens again and a Follow is re-sent to every account in the saved graph. Each
      one needs the far end to accept it, so this is a request rather than a restoration —
      some will not come back.
          
  
          
  
          
    
          A new keypair replaces the one in this home and the actor is republished so other
      servers learn it. Any other device still holding the old key stops being able to sign.
          
  
          
  
          
    
          This cannot be undone. A Delete goes to every follower's inbox telling
      those servers to drop the account and the posts they cached, the actor document is
      replaced with a Tombstone, and this agent will refuse to start again for this pod. Your
      posts and RDF stay on the pod; the identity does not come back.
          
    
          Standing down instead — keeping the handle, taking no mail — is
      bin/activitypod.mjs retire --keep-handle, and moving to another account is
      --move-to @you@elsewhere.
