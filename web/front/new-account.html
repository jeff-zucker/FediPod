FediPod - join the fediverse from a Solid pod.

Welcome to the FediPod.net gateway. Here, you can get a free pod and fediverse account or link to your existing pod/account. This gateway is a mail filter for accounts that stay yours. Your name, key and data live on your own pod; this box only receives your fediverse mail, verifies each delivery, drops spam and passes the rest to your pod.

FediPod is experimental and free to use. See the FediPod repository for details.

What do you want to do?

Create a new solo account

A group is an account people join: members post to it, and it carries each member's post to everyone in the group. Mastodon users can join with no pod of their own. A group needs a pod of its own.

Your account lives on a pod you own; this gateway filters its mail.

No pod yet? Any Solid pod host that offers https subdomains works, or a Community Solid Server you run yourself. Get the pod, then come back here.

This host also offers pods — you can take one instead of bringing your own; it stays exportable, so you can leave with everything.

Signing in proves the pod is yours — no password ever reaches this gateway.

Attach an existing FediPod account

Your account stays @you@your.pod — other servers keep finding you at your own address. Attaching only changes where your mail arrives: your actor advertises this gateway's door as its inbox, and everything that passes verification lands in your pod as before.

If your FediPod uses a custom root, give the full home URL (ending in /); a bare pod address assumes the default activitypods-js/.

Only the door is named here — it is a routing address for servers, not your handle.

Signing in proves the pod is yours — no password ever reaches this gateway.

Detach an existing FediPod account

Detaching is yours to do, not this gateway's — your agent republishes your actor with your pod's own inbox, and deliveries go straight there again. On your machine:

node bin/fedipod.mjs gateway --detach

Your name and data never moved, so nothing else changes. The door you had here goes quiet and its name can be reused.

Transfer a fediverse account to FediPod

The fediverse's standard migration works toward a FediPod: your followers move over automatically, and your old profile redirects. Posts stay on the old server — no fediverse migration carries them.

  1. Have a FediPod — create one first (the first option above).
  2. On your agent's admin page, set new followers to accepted automatically, so the wave of arriving followers doesn't queue.
  3. Tell your FediPod about the old account: 
    node bin/fedipod.mjs alias --add @you@old.server
  4. On the old server: Preferences → Account → Move to a different account, naming your FediPod address.
  5. Bring the rest over from the old server's CSV export: 
    node bin/fedipod.mjs import following_accounts.csv blocked_accounts.csv muted_accounts.csv lists.csv
